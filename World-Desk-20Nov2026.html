HEADLINE · MONEY

Anthropic opens 34% up, ends its first day 17% up, on a day the Dow drops 1,100 points

The artificial intelligence company Anthropic started trading on the Nasdaq on Thursday under the ticker ANT. Shares priced at $83 the night before first changed hands just before noon Eastern at about $111.50. The gain shrank through the afternoon and the stock closed at $97.17. Around it, the whole market fell hard: the Dow lost 2.2% and closed under 50,000 for the first time since May.
