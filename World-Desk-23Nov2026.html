HEADLINE · WAR

Ukraine says its sea drones sank a Russian warship in a Libyan port, far from the Black Sea

On Monday President Volodymyr Zelensky said seven Ukrainian-made sea drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi, Libya, early on Sunday. Satellite pictures show the ship capsized and mostly under water. Benghazi is about 1,900 km (1,200 miles) from Ukraine's coast. Ukraine has not said how the drones got there. Libya calls it a breach of its sovereignty; the United Nations (UN) mission wants an independent investigation.
