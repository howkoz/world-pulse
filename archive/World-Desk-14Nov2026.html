Starship Flight 15: the booster blew up in the Gulf, the ship is still in orbit and heading home for a tower catch

Ship 42, nicknamed "Don't Panic", reached orbit, released 26 Starlink satellites and has been circling Earth since Friday evening. If its final health check passes, it fires its engines over the Pacific, falls back through the atmosphere and tries to be caught by the launch tower at Starbase, Texas.

What happened to the booster

Booster 22 should have flown back and been caught by the tower 6.5 minutes after launch. Press play.

  • Flying back, 27 of its 28 engines lit.
  • It then detected a problem with its guidance, navigation and control (how it knows where it is and steers), so it switched to its backup plan: a practice catch over the sea instead of the tower.
  • It broke apart just before hitting the water. SpaceX has not published a cause.

CONTRADICTED

One website said a ship engine failed and the flight was cut to 3 hours

Only one live blog (drohnen.de) said this, and it turned out wrong. CNBC reports the ship circled the Earth 14 times, about 22 and a half hours, before the tower caught it.
