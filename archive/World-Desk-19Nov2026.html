HEADLINE · WORLD POLITICS

Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen; China and Russia publish a joint "Declaration on Peace"

Overnight, Eastern time, the three leaders held their first ever three-way meeting, at Kylin Villa in Shenzhen, China. It lasted about an hour to 90 minutes; accounts differ. China and Russia then released the same four-point text. The United States had not published its own version when this was checked. Trump flew home at about 6 am Eastern.
