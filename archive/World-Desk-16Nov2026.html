A Vulcan rocket failed 10 minutes into flight and 40 Amazon internet satellites were lost

United Launch Alliance (ULA) launched Vulcan from Cape Canaveral, Florida at 12:40 pm Eastern on Sunday. ULA says something went wrong about 10 minutes in and the satellites never reached orbit. It is the first total failure for ULA since the company began in 2006. Nobody knows the cause yet.
