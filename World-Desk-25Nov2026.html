Hackers hit a CIA intelligence system, and the Treasury's financial crime unit takes systems offline

On Tuesday the New York Times and CNN reported that hackers got into an unclassified system the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA), the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI) and the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA) use to study public information. Officials say no classified system was touched. The same day the Treasury's Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) said it found unauthorized access and switched some systems off. It comes days after the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) crime database hack. Nobody has claimed any of them.
