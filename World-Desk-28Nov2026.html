HEADLINE · WARS

Ukraine launches one of its biggest drone raids yet on Moscow, shutting the capital's airports

Overnight into Saturday 28 November, Ukraine sent a swarm of long-range drones toward the Moscow region. Moscow's mayor, Sergei Sobyanin, said about 462 drones came in and 123 were shot down near the capital. Flights were restricted at the two main Moscow airports, Vnukovo and Domodedovo, and at four more cities. Russia's Defence Ministry claimed it downed 1,246 Ukrainian drones across the whole country overnight. Ukraine, as usual, did not comment on the raid. Numbers on both sides are each party's own claim and are hard to check.
