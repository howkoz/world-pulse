HEADLINE · WEATHER

A Thanksgiving storm floods the Northeast and postpones the Macy's parade

A strong coastal storm climbed the East Coast on Thanksgiving Day, 26 November. The National Weather Service (NWS) warned of major coastal flooding and record high tides from the Southeast up to New England, with wind gusts up to about 65 miles per hour (mph) at the shore. All major airlines let travelers rebook for free. For the first time in years, the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York was pushed to Saturday because of the wind. Roughly 60 to 70 million people were under a flood threat.
