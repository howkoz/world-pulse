Nvidia brings Jensen Huang back as boss, and its stock jumps 31% in a day

Eleven days after he resigned, Nvidia's board voted unanimously on Monday to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, chief executive officer (CEO) and board director. The board says senior US government officials gave input and it found his return poses no national security risk; several outlets report President Trump pushed for it. The stock closed up 31.5%, adding nearly $1 trillion in value, and pulled Wall Street to its best day since April 2025.
