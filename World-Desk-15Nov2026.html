HEADLINE · IRAN AND THE MILITARY

Iran says it sent six drones at a US base in Bahrain, its second attack on the island in three days

Early Sunday, Bahrain sounded its first nationwide alert since September and told people to find shelter. Iran's Revolutionary Guards say they aimed at a US air defence radar at the Al-Riffa helicopter base. Bahrain says its air defences shot the attack down. Nobody has reported damage or injuries, and the US has not commented yet.
