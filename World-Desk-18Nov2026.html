HEADLINE · WORLD POLITICS

Xi Jinping meets Trump at the foot of his plane in Shenzhen; the three-way talks with Putin are on Thursday

Trump flew in from Manila on Wednesday afternoon, China time. Xi was waiting on a red carpet with troops and dancers. CNN says Xi has never before gone to the airport for a visiting US president. Putin also arrived in Shenzhen on Wednesday. The first meeting of the three leaders is set for Thursday 19 Nov; no hour has been announced. Meanwhile the Iran war spread to a fourth country: Iran says it fired drones at a US base in Kuwait.
