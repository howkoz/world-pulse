HEADLINE · IRAN AND MILITARY

Iran claims a third drone attack on a US radar in Bahrain in five days, as a third US aircraft carrier arrives

Before dawn on Tuesday, Iran's Revolutionary Guards said their drones hit a US-run radar at Sheikh Isa Air Base in Bahrain. Bahrain sounded a shelter alert, explosions were heard over the capital Manama, and Bahrain says it destroyed the attackers before they reached anything. No US statement yet. Near the Strait of Hormuz, a UK monitor reported two tankers with engine room fires on Monday.
