HEADLINE · MONEY

Nasdaq drops 5.3% and wipes out its 2026 gains as Nvidia falls 14%

Friday 20 Nov was the hardest day of this week's selloff. Reuters reported that the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), the US stock market regulator, is looking at Nvidia's accounting. Nvidia fell 13.8%. The tech-heavy Nasdaq followed, and the S&P 500 and the Dow both closed in a correction, at least 10% below their record highs. Overnight, bitcoin slid to its lowest price since September 2024.

What exactly the SEC is looking at

Reuters cites two people briefed. It could not say when the inquiry opened or which accounting issues it covers. The SEC declined to comment. An inquiry is not a finding of wrongdoing.

UNCONFIRMED

Russia sent weapons into Poland on purpose

Poland's prime minister calls it a likely provocation and a think tank, the Institute for the Study of War, assesses it was deliberate. Russia has not explained it. That is a judgement, not an established fact.
