HEADLINE · SPACE

United States regulators clear SpaceX for up to one million satellites to build data centres in space

On Wednesday and Thursday, 25 to 26 November, the Federal Communications Commission (FCC), the United States telecom regulator, gave SpaceX permission to deploy up to one million satellites to build computer data centres in orbit. SpaceX must launch at least one authorised space station by 2033, then add more gradually through 2040. For comparison, there are only about 12,000 working satellites in orbit today. Scientists are split: one astrophysicist called the plan "a fantasy", and astronomers worry about crowding and light pollution in the night sky.
